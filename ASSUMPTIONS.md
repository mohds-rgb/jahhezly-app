# Assumptions

Only assumptions required to keep the portfolio implementation coherent are recorded here.

1. Jahhezly V1 is a general retail Click & Collect system, not a healthcare or pharmacy workflow.
2. The two owner-authorized merchant names may appear in portfolio demo data, but the repository does not claim endorsement, partnership, revenue, or production deployment.
3. Paris Market has one known branch in Basra/Daraa based on owner-provided context.
4. cham-center has four branches; branch-specific locations beyond the owner-provided Kafr Sousa/Damascus context are intentionally not invented.
5. Product prices in demo data are illustrative portfolio data in SYP, not live store prices.
6. A 24-hour ready-for-pickup expiration is an engineering/demo default and requires business approval before production.
7. In-app/local notifications are implemented as a product mechanism; a production push provider is a future integration.
8. Android and iOS remain the intended Flutter targets; target-device verification is outside the current environment.
